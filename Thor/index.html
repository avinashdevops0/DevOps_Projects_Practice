THE GOD OF THUNDER

MIGHTY WARRIOR OF ASGARD

Thor Odinson is the Norse God of Thunder, son of Odin, and rightful heir to the throne of Asgard. Wielding the enchanted hammer Mjölnir, he commands the storm and protects the Nine Realms.

His strength knows no equal among the Æsir gods, capable of battling cosmic entities and unleashing lightning that can shatter mountains. Thor's journey from arrogant prince to worthy king is the stuff of legends.
